# 00 Brief

Write `brief.md`, one page, from the human's words and the brand pack (`brand:` in STATUS.md; read its `brand.md`). Read the project learnings (`learnings:` in STATUS.md; `route` prints them) and apply them in the brief.

- **Goal:** what the viewer should do or believe after the last slide.
- **Audience:** who they are, their situation, the words they use.
- **Format and size:** from STATUS.md (`carousel` or `image`; `portrait`, `square`, `story` or `fb-cover`). If the request needs another, ask; changing them means a new job.
- **Platform:** where it is posted (Facebook Page, Instagram, TikTok). On Facebook a carousel shows as a photo grid, not a swipe: the cover must work alone and the first few slides must make sense side by side.
- **Slides:** carousel: the slide count (a cover, one point per body slide, a closing CTA slide). Image: one.
- **Word limits:** the most words per slide (at most 30 per carousel slide and 12 on a single image unless the human sets lower).
- **CTA:** the one action, in the exact wording from `brand.md` or the human. Use only the page or handle names the human gives; never guess one.
- **Claim limits:** what we may and may not say. List the true numbers we can use. Never invent results, testimonials, quotes or real people.
- **Assets:** photos, screenshots and logos that exist (in the brand's `assets/` or supplied by the human), and what is missing.
- **Generated images:** which slides want a generated photo or illustration, or "none". Only if `brand.md` has an approved **Image style** section; without one, write "none". Never the creator's likeness or any real person.
- **References:** posts or files the human wants to match, if any.

If the goal, audience, CTA or claim limits are unclear, ask the human before writing.

Set `stage:` to the next stage and log one line.
