# 00 Brief

Write `brief.md`, one page, from the human's words and the brand pack (`brand:` in STATUS.md). Read the learnings `route` prints and apply them.

**Brand pack first.** If `<brand>/brand.md` does not exist, draft it with the human: voice, language, tone, offer wording, claim limits. Mark where each line came from, show it once and adjust it to their answer. They own it.

`brief.md` holds:

- **Horizon:** weeks and posts per week, from STATUS.md (`weeks:`, `per-week:`), and the start date.
- **Format mix:** posts per week by format, adding up to `per-week` (e.g. 3 reels, 2 carousels, 2 images). Formats: `reel`, `carousel`, `image`, `text` (`agentic-smm formats list`).
- **Offer:** what is sold, in the exact wording from `brand.md`.
- **CTA options:** follow, comment, save, share, and the offer CTA (e.g. a class booking, a pre-order, a studio enquiry), in brand wording.
- **Language:** e.g. English, Spanish, German, or a mix such as Spanglish or Hinglish, and anything never to mix in.
- **Posting slots:** days and times per week, with the timezone.
- **Goals:** numbers vs the baseline (`content/baseline.json` in the repo). No baseline yet: write `baseline: none` and set goals as raw numbers.
- **Production load:** how many pieces the human can make per shoot day and per design batch (defaults: 3–5 reels, 2–4 carousels, 4–6 images).
- **Score weights:** only if the human changes the topic weights in `03-trends`; otherwise "default".

Ask the human for anything missing. Never guess an offer, a slot or a number.

Set `stage:` to the next stage and log one line.
