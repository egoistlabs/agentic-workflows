# 04 Ideas

Make about 10 ideas per calendar slot (weeks × per-week from STATUS.md), then cut hard. `agentic-smm ideas <slug>` (the empty matrix, scoring and dedupe) arrives in build step 5; until then work by hand.

**Generate** in `ideas.md`. For each approved topic in `03-trends/topics.md`, cross angle types with hook patterns:

- Angles: `story`, `myth`, `mistake`, `how-to`, `comparison`, `numbers-breakdown`, `prediction`, `reaction`, `answer-a-comment`, `behind-the-scenes`, `list`, `framework`.
- Hooks: `number-first` ("3 loaves, 1 dough, 20 minutes"), `call-out` ("Still skipping leg day?"), `contrarian`, `confession`, `question-they-google`, `stakes` ("Skip this and your logo redesign costs twice as much…"), `pattern-interrupt`.

One line per idea: id (`i001`…), working title, angle, hook pattern, hook draft in the brand language, best format + alternates, format reason, the pain it answers (from `02-audience/audience.md`), a story id or `needs-story`, and the signal behind it.

**Choose the format** by what each format is best for (`agentic-smm formats list`). State the reason in a few words. A learning overrides the default.

**Score and cut.** Score each idea 1–5 on hook strength (specific, in their words, makes a promise right away), story fit, format fit and novelty. Drop ideas too close to anything in the repo's `content/used.md` or `content/backlog.md` (word overlap above 0.6). Shortlist the top ideas: enough for the series slots (all slots minus 1 fast-lane slot per week) plus a few spares, keeping the brief's format mix in view. Ideas from `timely` topics go in a separate **Fast lane** list with their expiry.

Write `shortlist.md`: the shortlisted ideas, each with its scores and format reason, then the fast lane.

CHECKPOINT: show the human `shortlist.md` and stop. After approval, add `approved: yes`, log their words in STATUS.md, add the unused ideas to the repo's `content/backlog.md` (score, formats, source signal, `status: new`, expiry if timely), and set the next stage.
