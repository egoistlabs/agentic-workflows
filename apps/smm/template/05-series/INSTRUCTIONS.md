# 05 Series

Group the approved shortlist (`04-ideas/shortlist.md`) into 2–5 series. Write `series.md`, one block per series:

- **Big topic:** one sentence in the audience's words, e.g. "Why my bread never rises like the bakery's" or "Why I keep quitting the gym in February".
- **Arc:** where the viewer starts, and where the last piece leaves them.
- **Pieces:** 3–8, in order, each with its idea id, working title and format. Formats can mix: story reels set up the problem, a carousel gives the framework to save.
- **Payoff:** the last piece, which usually carries the offer.

Rules:

- Every piece gives the viewer a reason to follow for the next one.
- At most 1 in 4 pieces pitches the offer. The rest end on follow, comment, save or share.
- Fill the series slots and match the brief's format mix. Timely ideas stay out of series: list them under **Fast lane** with their expiry.

CHECKPOINT: show the human `series.md` and stop. After approval, add `approved: yes`, log their words in STATUS.md, and set the next stage.
