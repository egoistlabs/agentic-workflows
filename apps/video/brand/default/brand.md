# Default brand

Used when a project has no brand pack of its own. A project pack only needs the tokens it wants to change; the rest come from here.

- **Voice:** plain, direct, second person. Short sentences. No hype words.
- **Claim limits:** state only what the human gives you. No numbers, results or testimonials unless they supply them.
- **CTA:** ask the human for the exact wording.
- **Accent serif (explainer, X6):** Instrument Serif Italic, SIL Open Font License (`assets/OFL-instrument-serif.txt`). A project pack can replace it with its own `fontAccent` / `fontAccentFile`.
