# 00 Brief

Write `brief.md`, one page, from the human's words and the brand pack (`brand:` in STATUS.md; read its `brand.md`). Read the project learnings (`learnings:` in STATUS.md; `route` prints them) and apply them in the brief.

- **Goal:** what the viewer should do or believe after watching.
- **Audience:** who they are, their situation, the words they use.
- **Language:** what is spoken in the recording, as a Whisper code (`en`, `tl`, `es`…). For mixed speech such as Spanglish, Hinglish or Taglish, use the non-English one (`es`, `hi`, `tl`); English words still come through.
- **Format and length:** from STATUS.md; the target length in seconds.
- **Offer / CTA:** the one action (R8). Exact wording from the brand pack.
- **Platform and CTA target** (talking-head, explainer): where it is posted (FB page / IG / TikTok) and the exact page or handle name the CTA points to. Ask; never guess a handle.
- **Speed** (talking-head, explainer): `Speed: 1.0` (default) or `Speed: 1.1`. Ask the human; never above 1.1. `agentic-video cut` reads this line.
- **Hero object and deliverable** (explainer): the visual metaphor the list or steps will live on (a ladder, a loop, a road, a contents list), if the human has one, and what the CTA hands over (a doc, a video, a program).
- **Claim limits:** what we may and may not say. List the true numbers we can use (R2). Never invent results, testimonials or real people.
- **Assets:** what exists (recordings, footage, screenshots, product UI, mascot, handouts) and what is missing.
- **References:** links or files the human wants to match, if any.

If the goal, audience or CTA is unclear, ask the human before writing.

Set `stage:` to the next stage and log one line.
