# 02 Story

Write `script.md` (talking-head: `structure.md`; explainer: `script.md` in its own table, below). This is the creative heart. Be bold, not safe.

**Concept first.** Pick one visual metaphor or idea that carries the whole piece and can turn into the CTA at the end. State it in one line at the top.

**Hook (R1, R2).** `Hook text:` 12 words or fewer, shown on screen from frame 0, containing a true number or a counted promise. The first VO line lands the same payoff in spoken form.

**Script table.** Two columns, one row per beat, 4–10 s each:

| Visuals / animation direction | VO / on-screen text |
| --- | --- |
| **0–3s. HOOK.** what is on screen, what moves, camera, SFX | **VO:** … <br>**On-screen:** … |

- Every row names its proof (R4): a real screenshot, UI, document or mechanism diagram. Never label invented examples "example" or "illustrative"; keep them generic, with numbers only from the brief.
- Plan something new every 1.3–3 s (R3); write the moves in the visual column.
- Write quotable contrast lines. Cut any sentence the viewer could predict.
- The last row is the CTA (R8): one action, held 3–5 s, nothing after it.

**Talking-head:** `structure.md` instead: the hook, the beats in order with timestamps from the raw footage once it exists, and the CTA. The rules are the talking-head group (T1–T8), not R4/R6/R7: the speaker carries the piece, and overlays act out lines rather than prove them. The hook's payoff is legible at frame 0 but usually carried natively (a notification showing the amount, the first caption), not by a title box. Name the open-loop object (T8): what the hook plants and how the payoff resolves it. Before footage exists, give the human a talk-through sheet to film from: a two-column table, `Beat | What to say (your own words)`, one short plain prompt per beat, only the hook (and any real number) quoted and marked *(say exactly)*, then 2–3 recording tips. If the brief came from agentic-smm, use its sheet as is.

**Explainer:** `script.md` is the script the human will record, so write it to be said aloud: short lines, dense, no pauses to fill. The rules are the explainer group (X1–X8), not R4/R6/R7.
- **Hook (X5):** `Hook text:` the claim, 12 words or fewer, with a number; then `Name:` the framework's name ("It's called the feedback ladder"), said by 5 s; then the count by 8 s.
- **Hero object (X2):** one line naming the visual metaphor, where it returns, and the recap before the CTA.
- **Table**, one row per line:

  | Beat | Line (spoken) | Job | Layout | Canvas build (element → word) | Hero |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | hook | "These are the 5 …" | what | canvas | title builds; proof collage → "five" | — |
  | hook | "It's called the feedback ladder." | so-what | face | — | — |
  | level-1 | "Level one is silence." | what | split | ladder, rung 1 lit → "one" | rung 1 |

  `Job` is `what` (names, definitions, structure, examples → `split`) or `so-what` (opinions, claims, payoffs → `face`). Every face line must be a judgement worth the punch-in. Aim for 15–40% face (X1).
- Each item gets a name, a one-line definition, one concrete example or analogy, and why it matters (to camera). Add one mid-video re-hook that raises the stakes for the back half, and a recap.
- The CTA row: "Comment '<keyword>' …", and the deliverable it shows (R8, explainer note).

Check against the brief's claim limits. Then CHECKPOINT: show the human the script and stop. After approval, add `approved: yes`, log their words, and set the next stage. If VO still has to be recorded, tell the human now, giving them the VO lines only. For an explainer, also give the recording notes in `03-source`.
