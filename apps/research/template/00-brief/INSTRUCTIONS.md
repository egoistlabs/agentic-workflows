# 00 Brief

Write two files. Keep each under one page.

**`brief.md` (neutral, read by every stage):** the research question, the target audience, the time window, and the budget.

- Write it from the user's own words only. Do not read the current project, its README, or its code to fill it in.
- The question names the audience and the topic, never our answer. Write "What problems do X hit when Y?", not "Do X need our feature?"
- `Target audience`: who they are, the words they use about themselves, and the tools they use. Use their vocabulary, not ours.
- No product name, company name, feature, or hypothesis. If the user mentioned any of these, put them in `sealed.md`.
- Leave `Source plan` empty. Stage 00b fills it in.

**`sealed.md` (read only by 04-decide):** what we are building, the decision this study informs, our hypotheses, and the user groups we are choosing between.

Check before moving on: read `brief.md` alone. If a stranger could guess our product or what we hope to find, rewrite it.

Set `stage: 00b-sources` in STATUS.md and log one line.
