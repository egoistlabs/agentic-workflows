# 04 Decide

Read `../03-cluster/clusters.md` and `../03-cluster/disconfirming.md` first. Only then open `../00-brief/sealed.md`: this is the first stage allowed to read it.

Do not write `decision.md` yet. Write `REVIEW.md` in two parts:

1. **What the market says**: the Sample section, the ranked cluster table, and the "cannot tell us" section, copied from `clusters.md` unchanged. Then the top disconfirming evidence.
2. **What it means for us** (interpretation, labelled as such):
   - First, for each hypothesis in `sealed.md`: supported, not supported, or no evidence, with the clusters behind it. If the data does not support our hypotheses, say so at the top.
   - The top three candidate targets (user, trigger moment, pain). For each one, say whether its evidence is firsthand or secondhand, with the share from `agentic-research count`.
   - Your recommendation.

Then stop and ask the human. (Checkpoint 2.)

After the human picks, log the pick in STATUS.md. Write `decision.md`: the target user, the trigger moment, the pain in their words, the demo story, and what would prove us wrong. Set `stage: done`.
